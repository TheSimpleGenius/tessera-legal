Delete your Tessera account

App: Tessera · Developer: TheSimpleGenius · Last updated: 29 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your Tessera account and everything stored with it. There are two routes: from inside the app, or by email if you can no longer reach the app.

Want to clear your logged data but keep your account? Jump to deleting some data without deleting your account.

Option 1 — delete from inside the app

  1. Open Tessera.
  2. Tap the settings icon in the top right of the calendar screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted.
Deletion is immediate and permanent. Your account and every synced record tied to it are removed from our servers straight away. There is no recovery period and no undo.

Option 2 — request deletion by email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email support.tessera@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Tessera account”.

We use the sending address to verify ownership, so a request from a different address cannot be actioned. Requests are honoured within 30 days.

No additional retention period applies to the deleted items. They are removed when the deletion runs, not queued for later cleanup.

Data kept on your device

Deleting your account does not touch the copy of your data stored locally on your phone — that copy is yours, and Tessera works fully offline without an account. To remove it as well:

  • Settings → Clear logging history deletes your logs, field values and journal entries from the device (and from our servers, while you are still signed in), or
  • Uninstall the app, which removes all of its local storage.

Delete some data without deleting your account

If you want to clear your logged history but keep your account, use Settings → Clear logging history inside the app. While you are signed in this removes the data from our servers as well as from your device. Your account, and any activities you have defined, remain.

To delete the account itself instead, see the options at the top of this page.

Bug reports

A report you send from Settings → Report a bug while signed in is linked to your account and deleted with it. A report sent while signed out is not linked to any account, so deleting an account cannot find it: email support.tessera@gmail.com with roughly when you sent it and what it said, and we will delete it. Either way, the hashed IP address stored with each report to limit repeat reports is cleared after 90 days.

Crash reports

Crash reports are technical diagnostics (stack trace, device model, OS version) collected by Sentry in release builds only. They are configured to exclude personally identifying information and contain none of your logging data, so they are not linked to your account and are not affected by account deletion. They age out under Sentry's own retention schedule.
