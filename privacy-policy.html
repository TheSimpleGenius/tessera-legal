Tessera Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Tessera is a personal habit- and event-logging calendar app. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it lives, and your choices.

Summary

  • Local-first. All your logging data is stored on your device. You can use every feature of Tessera without creating an account.
  • Optional sync. If you create an account, your data is synced to our backend (Supabase) so it can follow you across devices.
  • Usage analytics, never content. From an upcoming update, the app records which features are used, never what you log or write. You can switch this off.
  • No ads, no data sales. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data the app stores

On your device (always):

  • Activities you define (names, icons, fields, targets)
  • Daily logs and field values you enter
  • Journal entries, including any images you attach
  • App preferences (theme, display settings)

This data is stored in the app's private storage and is not accessible to other apps.

On our servers (only if you sign in):

  • Your email address (used as your account identifier)
  • The same activities, logs, journal text, and journal images as above, synced so they are available on your other devices

Sync data is stored with Supabase. Every record is scoped to your account: row-level security ensures no other user can read or modify your data. Journal images are stored in a private bucket accessible only to your account via short-lived signed URLs.

Crash reports (release builds only):

If the app crashes, a technical crash report (stack trace, device model, OS version) may be sent to Sentry to help us fix bugs. Crash reports are configured to exclude personally identifying information; your logging data is never included.

Bug reports (only if you send one):

If you send a report from Settings → Report a bug, it reaches us with the message you write, the app version and build, your phone’s manufacturer and model, its Android version, whether you were signed in, and the state of sync at the time. Nothing you have logged or written elsewhere in the app is included. You can send a report without an account; if you are signed in, it is linked to your account. If you put your email address in the message, we may use it to reply.

To stop a faulty app or a script flooding us, each IP address can send ten reports a day. To count them, we store a hashed form of the address a report came from, not the address itself, and clear it after 90 days. Reports are stored with Supabase and emailed to the developer once a week through Resend.

Usage analytics (on unless you switch it off):

Starting with the update that introduces it, the app records which screens you open and which features you use, and when (for example, opening the app, logging an activity, or writing in the journal), along with the app version. These records never include what you log or write: no activity or field names, no values, no journal text and no photos. We use them only to see which features are used and whether people keep using Tessera, so we know what to improve.

Each record carries a random ID that the app creates the first time it runs. The ID identifies the installation, not you. While you are signed in, records are also linked to your account. Records are sent whether or not you have an account, until you turn them off in Settings → Share usage analytics. They are stored with Supabase, are never used for advertising, sold or shared, and are deleted 13 months after they are recorded.

What happens when you sign in

Apart from crash reports, usage analytics and any bug report you choose to send (all above), nothing leaves your device until you sign in. At that point:

  • If your account has no data yet, the data already on this device is uploaded to it.
  • If your account already has data and this device has data of its own, Tessera asks you which to keep before uploading anything. Choosing “Keep both” uploads this device’s data and combines it with your account’s; choosing “Use my account’s data” replaces the copy on this device and uploads nothing; choosing “Cancel” signs you back out, uploads nothing, and leaves this device’s data unchanged.
  • While you are signed out, or if you chose “Use without an account”, none of your logging data is uploaded at all.

Signing out stops syncing. It does not delete anything, on the device or on our servers.

Sign-in options

  • Email + password — handled by Supabase Auth. Passwords are hashed; we never see them.
  • Google Sign-In — we receive only your email address and basic profile from Google. We do not access your contacts, calendar, or other Google data.

What we do NOT collect

  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos (other than images you explicitly attach to journal entries), or other device data beyond the model and Android version in a crash or bug report

Data retention and deletion

  • Local data stays on your device until you delete it (Settings → Clear logging history) or uninstall the app.
  • Account deletion is available in-app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, all synced records, all uploaded images, any bug reports you sent while signed in, and the usage analytics recorded while you were signed in from our servers. It cannot be undone. Data on your device is kept unless you clear it separately.
  • Bug reports are kept until they have been dealt with, and the hashed IP address on each is cleared after 90 days. A report sent while signed out is not linked to any account, so account deletion cannot find it: email us roughly when you sent it and what it said, and we will delete it.
  • Usage analytics are deleted 13 months after they are recorded. Those recorded while you were signed out carry only the random ID, which does not identify you, so account deletion cannot find them; they are deleted on the same schedule.
  • Sign out stops syncing but keeps both local and server data intact.

Delete your Tessera account

To delete your account and all data associated with it (activities, logs, journal entries, uploaded images, bug reports sent while signed in, and usage analytics recorded while signed in):

  1. Open Tessera and go to Settings → Delete account, then confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  2. If you can no longer access the app, email support.tessera@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my Tessera account”. Requests are honoured within 30 days.

In both cases every synced record, image, bug report and usage record tied to your account is permanently removed from our servers. Data stored locally on your device is unaffected until you clear it or uninstall the app.

Children

Tessera is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
